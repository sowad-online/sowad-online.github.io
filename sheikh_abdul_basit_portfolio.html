Sampling Lifecycle

Sample Development & Approval Stages

From initial proto to final ex-factory shipment, maintaining clear approval logs is the core of sample follow-up.

STAGE 01

Proto Sample

Evaluates aesthetic silhouette and preliminary styling using available fabric quality.

Focus: Design & Silhouette
STAGE 02

Fit Sample

Strict measurement validation on mannequin/live models. Log buyer fit comments into CAD.

Focus: Measurements & Tolerances
STAGE 03

Size Set Sample

Verifies proportional grading across the full size curve (XS to 3XL) before bulk cutting.

Focus: Grading Balance
STAGE 04

PP Sample (Pre-Production)

Made with 100% actual bulk fabric and production trims. Mandatory benchmark for the PP meeting.

Focus: Bulk Quality Standard
STAGE 05

TOP (Top of Production)

Pulled from first 100 bulk sewn garments to ensure production mirrors the signed PP sample.

Focus: Sewing Line Replication
STAGE 06

Shipment / Retention Sample

Randomly pulled from final packed cartons for buyer record keeping and factory archive.

Focus: Final Pack Audit
